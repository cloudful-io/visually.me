The Timeline View is another step towards fulfilling the original mission of Visually.Me.  As a person looking forward to retirement, one would often ask: When does everything happen?  This is exactly the reason why the Timeline view is a highly-sought feature.  The Timeline View, embedded on your [dashboard](/dashboard), is a visual, chronological representation of your financial life. It shows key milestones such as when you start drawing from retirement savings, when pensions begin, or when Social Security kicks in. They are plotted against the years that matter most to you.  Instead of scanning rows of numbers, you can immediately see how your income sources unfold over time.

More is yet to come!  So far, we have incorporated all of your important milestones with respect to your retirement income and investment in the Timeline view.  Up next is your anticipated mortgage payoff milestone.
