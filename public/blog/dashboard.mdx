Today marks the largest and most significant update to Visually.Me since its initial debut. With this release, Visually.Me evolves from a simple calculation tool into a personalized retirement planning companion.

For the first time, users can create an account, log in, and securely save all of their income sources in one place. Whether you have a retirement savings, social security, or pension, Visually.Me now brings everything together to forecast your long-term retirement outlook.

A brand-new dashboard provides a clear, intuitive snapshot of your financial future. You’ll see your projected annual income at your target retirement age, how it compares to the previous year, and the estimated value of your retirement investment accounts like 401(k)s or TSP. These views are designed to help you understand your readiness at a glance—without needing spreadsheets or complex calculators.

You can add as many income sources as you’d like, and Visually.Me adapts in real time. The more information you enter, the more complete and accurate your projection becomes, giving you confidence in the road ahead.

At this stage, pension calculations are available only for Federal employees, but expanding support to other pension systems is already on the roadmap. This is just the beginning—many powerful features are in development to help make retirement planning simple, visual, and accessible for everyone.
