We’re excited to introduce the updated Real Estate Card, with new information that makes it easy to see the financial performance of your properties—right on your dashboard.

With the Real Estate Card, you can quickly view:

- **Monthly Income:** Total rental income generated by the property.

- **Monthly Expenses:** Mortgage, property tax, insurance, HOA fees, and other recurring costs.

- **Net Cash Flow:** The difference between income and expenses, highlighted for quick insights.

Each card displays the property’s label, address, and type, and provides a link to view full details for deeper analysis. Positive net cash flow is clearly marked, helping you identify which properties are performing well at a glance.

This feature streamlines property management by giving you a clear, real-time snapshot of each property’s financial health, so you can make informed decisions quickly.
