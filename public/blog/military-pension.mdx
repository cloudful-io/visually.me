We’re excited to introduce a brand-new feature that brings [Uniformed Service Pension calculator](/calculators/military-pension) to the platform! Whether you’re simply curious about your estimated military pension or looking to integrate it into your long-term retirement planning, this update makes it effortless.

This first release supports the two most common military retirement systems:

- High-36 (High-3)
- Blended Retirement System (BRS)

These plans are fully integrated into our calculation engine, offering simple, day-to-day estimates for casual users, as well as the ability for authenticated users to add uniformed service pension as an income source directly into their personalized dashboards.

This is just the beginning—future releases will expand support for more pension types and customization options. Stay tuned for continued enhancements!
