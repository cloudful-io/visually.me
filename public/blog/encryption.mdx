At Visually.Me, your privacy is our top priority.  We understand that your personal data, like year of birth and retirement goals, is sensitive.  That’s why we use robust, application-level encryption to keep your information secure.

Every piece of data is protected using strong cryptographic keys that are carefully managed at the application level. We employ key rotation practices to ensure your data stays secure over time, without impacting your experience. Encryption is applied seamlessly, so your access to features remains smooth and intuitive. Only you can decrypt your personal information, keeping it safe from unauthorized access. Our security practices follow industry best standards to protect against potential threats.

With Visually.Me, safeguarding your sensitive information is built into every feature. Enjoy the full benefits of our platform knowing your privacy and data security are always a top priority.
