This is the largest update since the initial release of Visually.Me.  Users can now login and store their different sources of income to project their retirement outlook.  The brand new dashboard shows users their annual income at their target retirement age, and comparing it to their income in the prior year.  It also shows their retirement investment account (e.g. 401k) balance.  It allows users to add as many or as few income sources as they feel comfortable projecting.  The more they input, the more complete picture it shows.  At this moment, pension calculation is limited to only Federal employees, but I plan to expand this to other pension types in the future.
