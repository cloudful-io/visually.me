# Real-Estate Property Management

## Purpose
**[Verified]** Let authenticated users save and manage property assumptions and review individual or combined projected cash flow. The property calculation details are documented in [Real-Estate Property Projection](../calculators/real-estate-projection.md).

## User
**[Inferred]** A homeowner or landlord tracking projected rental income and recurring ownership costs, optionally for a spouse.

## Inputs
**[Verified]** A property record has a required name, optional address, optional user/spouse owner, property type, and real-estate projection assumptions. Fields and model rules are detailed in [Real-Estate Property Projection](../calculators/real-estate-projection.md). [Property editor](../../../src/app/%28DashboardLayout%29/components/dashboard/EditDialogs/EditRealEstateDialog.tsx)

## Outputs
**[Verified]** Property cards show current-year monthly income, expenses, and net cash flow, with a link to the detail projection. The Real Estate page can display combined net cash flow or annual income and expenses, plus a combined yearly table. The detail page offers Summary and Detail charts and a projection table. [Property card](../../../src/app/%28DashboardLayout%29/components/dashboard/RealEstateCard.tsx) · [Real Estate page](../../../src/app/%28DashboardLayout%29/real-estate/page.tsx)

## Workflow
**[Verified]** A user adds a property from the Real Estate page, opens a property card to view its projection, then edits assumptions or supported yearly values. Deleting a property requires confirmation. Property records are loaded and saved through `/api/assets`; the page preserves existing year overrides when base assumptions are changed. [Property list](../../../src/app/%28DashboardLayout%29/components/dashboard/RealEstateDetailedList.tsx) · [asset API](../../../src/app/api/assets/route.ts)

## Business rules
**[Verified]** The Real Estate page combines loaded properties by calendar year. Per-property values are generated by the calculation engine described in the linked projection document. The spouse inclusion setting is shared with the Income and Investment views.

**[Confirmed product decision]** Real Estate remains a separate navigation area and a distinct asset type from Income and Investment.

## Edge cases
**[Verified]** With no properties, the page shows the add-property workflow without combined charts or table. A property card uses the current year's projection row for its headline amounts; if no current-year row is present, the displayed amounts default to zero.

## Dependencies
**[Verified]** Uses `financial-calcs`, the property asset registry, user/spouse attributes, `/api/assets`, Supabase asset storage, and shared chart/table components. The service uses the unified `assets` table, although SQL also contains a legacy `real_estate` table. [Asset registry](../../../src/lib/assets/registry.ts) · [asset service](../../../src/lib/assets/service.ts) · [schema](../../../sql/schema.sql)

## Current limitations
**[Verified]** Property type is saved but has no effect on the projection formulas. The checked-in `assets` schema does not declare the `spouse` column used by the service. See [Saved calculator projections](../calculators/saved-projections.md) for this schema discrepancy and the encrypted asset workflow.

## Evidence and questions
**[Needs human confirmation]** The checked-in schema omits the `spouse` field used by the asset service. The product owner indicates the deployed schema may also be missing fields, but that has not been confirmed against the deployed database.
