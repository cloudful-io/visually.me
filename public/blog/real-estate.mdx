Real estate can be one of the most powerful—and complex—parts of your financial picture. Rental income, mortgages, taxes, insurance, maintenance, and long-term appreciation all play a role, and it’s easy for the details to become fragmented across spreadsheets and apps.

**Visually.Me brings everything together in one clear, visual experience.**

Visually.Me lets you manage each real estate property as a first-class part of your financial plan.

For every property, you can track:

- Rental income
- Mortgage payments
- Property taxes
- Insurance
- Key milestones, such as payoff year

Visually.Me projects property income and expenses over time, helping you see:

- When a property is cash-flow positive
- How rising expenses affect long-term returns
- What happens after a mortgage is paid off
- How rental income contributes to retirement cash flow

Instead of guessing, you can see how the income and expense of a property evolves year by year.  Try it out today: [Real Estate Properties](/real-estate)
